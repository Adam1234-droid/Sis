SIA Door Supervisor Licence

If you want to legally work as a Door Supervisor or Security Guard in the United Kingdom, the SIA Door Supervisor licence is essential. Oaktree B|S|P supports you from training through to application so you can take frontline roles legally.

A Door Supervisor licence also lets you work as a Security Guard. Renew every 3 years with our Top-Up Refresher.
