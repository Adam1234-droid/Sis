Get your SIA licence after training

The SIA issues licences for frontline private security roles. Complete the right course, then apply.

Door Supervisor Licence

Doors, events, retail and guarding. Most flexible licence.

Security Guard Licence

Static guarding at offices, shops and warehouses.

CCTV Licence

Public Space Surveillance / control room work.

Close Protection Licence

VIP and executive protection roles.

The process

  1. Enrol on an SIA-approved Oaktree course.
  2. Pass the exams and practical assessments.
  3. Apply for your licence on the SIA website (identity + criminal record check). Licence fee is paid to the SIA, currently £204, and is valid for 3 years.
