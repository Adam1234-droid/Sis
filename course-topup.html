Door Supervisor units

  • Unit 1: Principles of Working as a Door Supervisor (Refresher) — 25/35, 55 minutes

First Aid requirement

You must hold a valid Emergency First Aid qualification (valid at least 12 months). If you don’t, book our First Aid course alongside this refresher.

Entry: you must have held an SIA licence within the last 3 years and have sufficient English skills.
