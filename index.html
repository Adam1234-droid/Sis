What is an SIA licence, and why do I need one?

An SIA (Security Industry Authority) licence is a mandatory certification for people working in specific private security roles in the UK. Holding this licence shows you meet the training and professionalism standards required by law. Oaktree is an SIA-approved training centre delivering Pearson-approved licence-linked qualifications.

How do I get SIA training?

Book and complete an SIA-approved course that matches your role — Door Supervisor, Security Guard, CCTV or Close Protection. After you pass, you can apply for your SIA licence. Call 0330 175 9933 or book online.

How long does SIA training take?

Door Supervisor training is typically around 6 days (about 45 hours). CCTV is 1 day. Close Protection is 16–21 days. Emergency First Aid is 6 hours. Top-up refresher is 2 days for Door Supervisors and 1 day for Security Officers.

How much does SIA training cost?

Door Supervisor from £350, CCTV from £185, Close Protection from £1,299, Top-Up Refresher from £150, First Aid from £48.88, Train the Trainer from £1,500. If you find the same course cheaper elsewhere, Oaktree will match it and beat it by 10%.

How do I apply for an SIA licence after training?

Once you have your qualification, apply on the official SIA website. You will complete identity checks and a criminal record check. The SIA licence fee is paid separately to the SIA (currently £204). Oaktree guides you through every step.

How long is an SIA licence valid for?

An SIA licence is valid for 3 years. Renew it with up-to-date training — our Top-Up Refresher courses are designed for this.

Can I work as a Security Guard with a Door Supervisor Licence?

Yes. A Door Supervisor licence allows you to work as both a Security Guard and a Door Supervisor. A Security Guard licence does not permit door work.
