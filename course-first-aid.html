First Aid requirement for an SIA licence

SIA Door Supervisors and Security Guards need valid Emergency First Aid.

£48.88

6 hours · Town Meadows, Rochdale

Before enrolling on Door Supervisor or Top-Up Refresher training you must hold a valid Emergency First Aid qualification. We run Emergency First Aid alongside the Top-Up course so you can complete everything in one booking.

If you already hold Emergency First Aid or higher, it must be valid for at least 12 months from the start of your SIA course. Bring the certificate for your tutor on the day.
