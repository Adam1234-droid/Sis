What is the SIA?

Your trusted partner for SIA security courses and licensing.

The Security Industry Authority (SIA) is the regulator of the UK’s private security industry. It was established in 2003 as an independent body sponsored by the Home Office.

What it does

  • Licensing — people in specified roles must hold a valid licence
  • Approved Contractor Scheme — voluntary quality scheme for companies
  • Enforcement — action against unlicensed operators

Licence categories

  • Security guarding
  • Door supervision
  • Close protection
  • Cash and valuables in transit
  • Public space surveillance (CCTV)
  • Key holding
