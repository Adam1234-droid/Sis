Oaktree Academy empowers employers and learners in security, events and public safety.

Mission

We are committed to inspirational learning that forges remarkable futures. We empower individuals, communities and employers with learning that enhances social mobility and economic success.

Vision

To be recognised as a top-tier provider of career-focused education, collaborating with industry partners to meet the needs of the workforce.

Values

Learners first. Innovation. Excellence in execution. Diversity. Collective success. Ethical integrity. Sustainable development. We also work to empower women in a traditionally male-dominated market.

At Oaktree B|S|P we deliver security training nationwide. Our trainers have helped thousands of people. We also offer security guard hire for short- and long-term contracts, and we help sole traders and businesses generate and conserve money.
